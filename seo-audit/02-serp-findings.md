## 1. Competitive SERP Intent Landscape

We evaluated the top search results for high-intent B2B assessment conversion and QTI queries:

### Query Cluster 1: `assessment content digitization services` / `assessment conversion services`
- **Dominant Ranking Types:** Legacy BPO firms (e.g., MPS Limited, Aptara, Integra Software) offering generic digital publishing and editorial workflows.
- **Competitor Deficiencies:**
  - They treat assessment content as generic eBook or typesetting conversions.
  - No deep technical focus on QTI 2.1 / 3.0 packaging, interactive item types, or automated validation engines.
  - High friction to engagement (opaque pricing, lengthy RFP requirements).
- **Husimi Opportunity & Differentiation:**
  - Position Husimi as specialized assessment engineers who understand STEM content, formula rendering (LaTeX/MathML), and LMS compatibility.
  - Low-risk "Send a Sample" / pilot conversion mechanism.

### Query Cluster 2: `QTI conversion services` / `QTI 2.1 vs QTI 3.0`
- **Dominant Ranking Types:** 1EdTech consortium official specifications, academic whitepapers, and occasional platform documentation (Canvas, Moodle, Learnosity).
- **Competitor Deficiencies:**
  - Official specs are dense (100+ pages) and don't provide practical migration workflows or troubleshooting tips for real assessment operations teams.
- **Husimi Opportunity & Differentiation:**
  - Provide actionable, authentic guides on `/resources` (e.g., real QTI XML samples, Canvas import caveats, formula delimiter compatibility).

### Query Cluster 3: `Canvas quiz migration services` / `LMS question bank migration`
- **Dominant Ranking Types:** University IT knowledge-base articles (Canvas Community forums) explaining how manual export/import fails.
- **Competitor Deficiencies:**
  - Educational institutions struggle with questions losing images, equations breaking, and questions converting into uneditable blocks.
- **Husimi Opportunity & Differentiation:**
  - Speak directly to assessment platform operations teams and publishers who need bulk migration with guaranteed equation/asset preservation.

## 2. Page Hierarchy & Internal Linking Rules

1. **Every page must have one primary intent**:
   - Informational guides on `/resources` link to commercial `/services` and `/contact`.
   - Technical proof pages (`/resources/sample-package` and `/resources/source-pdf`) prove the capabilities described on `/services`.
2. **Anchor text must be descriptive**:
   - Avoid generic "click here" or "learn more".
   - Use "Explore QTI conversion services", "Inspect the QTI 3.0 proof package", "Discuss your migration project".
