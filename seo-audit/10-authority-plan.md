**Date:** 2026-10-15  
**Auditor:** Senior SEO Engineer / Full-Stack Developer (Antigravity)

## 1. Principles
- **No Purchased Backlinks:** Google Search spam policies penalize artificial link networks and paid link schemes.
- **Genuine Utility:** Earn references by solving hard technical problems in digital assessment (e.g., LaTeX rendering failures in Canvas, QTI 3.0 packaging quirks, MathML entity mapping).
- **Accurate Claims:** Never claim partnerships (e.g., "Official Canvas Partner" or "Pearson Certified") unless an actual formal certification/partnership agreement exists.

- **URL:** `/resources` / technical guide
- **Audience:** Instructional designers, university IT departments, EdTech content managers.
- **Problem Solved:** Canvas parses `\(...\)` vs `$$...$$` LaTeX math delimiters differently depending on the quiz engine (Classic Quizzes vs New Quizzes). Providing a free reference guide and conversion test case gives immediate utility to teams struggling with broken formulas.

### Asset 2: QTI 3.0 Standard Validation Checklist
- **URL:** `/resources/sample-package`
